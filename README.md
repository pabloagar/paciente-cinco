**English** · [Leer en español](README.es.md)

**Short clinical cases. One decision at a time.**

An educational beta for practicing clinical reasoning through guided cases, brief activities and feedback, with progress saved on the device.

## What you can try

- A guided first consultation, with history, examination and decisions.
- Practice activities involving clinical decisions, schematic ECGs and respiratory sounds.
- Prepared feedback and references, a learning map, review and local progress.
- Resuming activities at your own pace; sessions are designed to be brief, not timed examinations.

## Implemented and planned

The guided introduction and available practice stations are playable. “General de zona” and “Hospitalario” are shown as **in preparation**; they are not complete learning pathways. Content review, physical phone testing and evaluation of learning outcomes remain pending.

## Scope and technology

The development code stays private; this repository contains the public presentation and screenshots only. The app uses React, TypeScript and Vite, with IndexedDB for local progress. There are no accounts or cross-device synchronization.

Answers and feedback are prepared content, not live generative chat. ECG traces are schematic, not diagnostic recordings. Review requests stay local and can be exported; they are not automatically sent to a server. The beta does not certify competence or guide real patient care.
